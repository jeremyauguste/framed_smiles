Our Story

Get to know the passionate team behind Framed Smiles and our commitment to capturing your precious moments.

Welcome to Framed Smiles

Founded in 2010, Framed Smiles began with a simple vision: to create a photography studio where every client feels like family. What started as a small home-based business has grown into one of Orlando's most beloved photography studios.

Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that the best photographs come from genuine connections. We take the time to understand your story, your personality, and what makes your relationships special. This approach allows us to capture authentic moments that truly reflect who you are.

Over the years, we've had the privilege of documenting thousands of weddings, family milestones, and special occasions. Each session is a new opportunity to create art that will be treasured for generations.
