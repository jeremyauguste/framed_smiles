Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2-3 weeks in advance for portrait sessions and 6-12 months for weddings. However, we do accommodate last-minute requests when possible.

What should we wear?

We'll send you a detailed style guide after booking! Generally, we recommend coordinating colors (not matching), avoiding busy patterns, and choosing timeless outfits you feel confident in.

What if it rains on our outdoor session day?

Don't worry! We monitor the weather closely and will reach out to reschedule if needed. Alternatively, we can move to our studio or a covered location.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, a 50% deposit is required to secure your date. The remaining balance is due on the day of your session.
